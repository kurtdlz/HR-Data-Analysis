# HR Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to identify patterns and factors associated with **employee attrition, job satisfaction, workload, salary, and workforce distribution**.

The analysis was developed in **Microsoft Power BI** as an interactive HR analytics dashboard designed to help HR teams and decision-makers better understand workforce trends and potential employee retention concerns.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall employee attrition and retention
* Identify departments and role levels with higher attrition
* Examine the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition
* Compare workload across departments
* Analyze job satisfaction in relation to weekly working hours
* Explore salary patterns across different workload levels
* Provide an interactive dashboard for HR-related decision-making

---

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides an overview of important HR metrics, including:

* **Total Employees**
* **Active Employees**
* **Attrition Count**
* **Attrition Rate**
* **Average Salary**
* **Average Job Satisfaction**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the organization's workforce and retention situation.

### Attrition Analysis

The dashboard examines attrition from several perspectives:

* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Role Level
* Attrition by Job Satisfaction

This helps identify workforce segments where employee turnover may require further investigation.

### Workload Analysis

Workload-related analysis includes:

* Average weekly working hours by department
* Job satisfaction across different weekly-hour levels
* Projects handled in relation to salary

These views help explore whether workload and employee satisfaction may be associated with workforce outcomes.

### Interactive Filtering

A **Department slicer** allows users to filter the dashboard and investigate HR metrics for specific departments.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

The dashboard highlights several areas that can be investigated from an HR perspective:

* Employee attrition can vary considerably across departments and role levels.
* Job satisfaction provides an important dimension for understanding employee turnover.
* Differences in weekly working hours can be compared across departments and against job satisfaction.
* Salary patterns can be examined alongside workload indicators such as projects handled.
* Combining attrition, satisfaction, workload, and compensation provides a broader view of potential employee retention factors.

> **Note:** The dashboard is intended for exploratory analysis. Relationships observed in the data should not automatically be interpreted as causal relationships.
